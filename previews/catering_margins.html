How this was computed — assumptions

Revenue = catering payment lines per event in the guest area (net of 13% VAT). Food = grocery & supplier purchases from the expense sheet, matched to each event by the team's expense notes first, then by date window (during the stay, or up to 6 days before check-in). Bar stock, ice, team meals and equipment are excluded. Staff = actual paid days for Slink (chef €200 × 6d + service €120 × 5d); for events without recorded staff costs, the same pattern is applied: chef €200 × (nights + 1) + service €120 × nights (marked *). Energy & usage = 6% of catering revenue, per hospitality-sector norms (energy 3–5% + consumables/wear 1–2%) — deliberately not taken from the expense sheet to avoid double counting utilities.

⚠ Caveats: Our Suite Life food (€11/guest-night) looks under-captured — May purchases were rarely labelled; true margin is likely lower. Slink food misses the August shopping (sheet ends 31 Jul) — true margin is lower than shown. Ilo Retreat and Tommy & friends had a paid kitchen team (€2,440 est. / €2,120 actual) and food (€1,416 / €1,698) but no catering revenue line — catering was folded into the rental price or not billed. Worth fixing before drawing conclusions on those two.
